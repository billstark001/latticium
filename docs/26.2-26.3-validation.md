## Acceptance work still open

- Exercise finite fill, replace, and clear commands in an actual world on all four main targets, including survival inventory consumption, reach failures, break denial, and observation after server updates.
- Exercise active schematic placements on each of the three bridge targets: exact target, default ignored air, explicit `include_air`, overlap conflicts, placement removal and switch, and old-session invalidation.
- Confirm that a main JAR launches without its bridge and that a disabled profile has no scan or action cost in each loader.
- Measure section capture and scan budgets in a larger loaded world. The current implementation eagerly captures section facts and does not have a performance benchmark.
- Exercise chunk unload and reload during scanning and while an action receipt is pending; verify deferred sections resume and receipts time out.
- Exercise an unloaded neighbor needed by `surface()` or `adjacent(...)`, then load it; verify the unknown section is rescanned while initial scanning continues.
- Scan a `replace` candidate, change its source block before the first action, and verify the old candidate is skipped. Interrupt a break-then-place replacement after its confirmed break and verify a different intervening state blocks replanning.
- Defer the second step of a known multi-step interaction after confirming the first; verify the frozen target resumes only if the last confirmed state remains present.
- Give a replacement only one remaining action and verify it does not break the existing block. Check that reachable snow-layer or repeater changes use their ordinary interaction before any break fallback.
- Exhaust the planner's node limit on a candidate with a possible non-breaking path and verify the job defers without starting a break. Make a modded placement predictor fail and verify the job reports a deferral without crashing the client tick.
- Try switching jobs while an action receipt is pending; verify the old job continues and the new start reports the pending action. Try an invalid inline command profile and verify it does not replace the saved profile.
- Start a new blueprint preview while a blueprint job is active, then change or remove the placement; verify the job keeps its own snapshot and reports stale targets.
- Verify all generated JARs in an ordinary modpack installation, beyond Gradle development runs.

## Unsupported or conservative behavior

The placement oracle covers ordinary hotbar `BlockItem` placement, layered snow, repeater delay interaction, and ordinary block breaking. It does not model arbitrary modded placement, entity actions, redstone evolution, gravity, waterlogging transitions, or multi-block effects; those positions are blocked or deferred. The schematic bridges report any overlapping subregions as a conflict, including overlaps whose target states may be identical. A change to schematic contents that leaves placement bounds unchanged may not invalidate a frozen target. Query and preview are bounded partial reads, not full-domain aggregate results. These gaps prevent claiming the complete 26.2/26.3 acceptance matrix.
