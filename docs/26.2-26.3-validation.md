- Exercise active schematic placements on each of the three bridge targets: exact target, default ignored air, explicit `include_air`, overlap conflicts, placement removal and switch, and old-session invalidation.
- Confirm that a main JAR launches without its bridge and that a disabled profile has no scan or action cost in each loader.
- Measure section capture and scan budgets in a larger loaded world. The current implementation eagerly captures section facts and does not have a performance benchmark.
- Verify all generated JARs in an ordinary modpack installation, beyond Gradle development runs.

## Unsupported or conservative behavior

The placement oracle covers ordinary hotbar `BlockItem` placement, layered snow, repeater delay interaction, and ordinary block breaking. It does not model arbitrary modded placement, entity actions, redstone evolution, gravity, waterlogging transitions, or multi-block effects; those positions are blocked or deferred. The schematic bridges report any overlapping subregions as a conflict, including overlaps whose target states may be identical. A change to schematic contents that leaves placement bounds unchanged may not invalidate a frozen target. Query and preview are bounded partial reads, not full-domain aggregate results. These gaps prevent claiming the complete 26.2/26.3 acceptance matrix.
