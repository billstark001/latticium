## Unsupported or conservative behavior

The placement oracle covers ordinary hotbar `BlockItem` placement, layered snow, repeater delay interaction, and ordinary block breaking. It does not model arbitrary modded placement, entity actions, redstone evolution, gravity, waterlogging transitions or multi-block effects. Dry breaking can be predicted as air, but fluid-containing blocks and directly coupled multi-block structures defer before breaking. Destructive exact replacement currently requires a verifiable plain property-free block on a plain full-block support; property-sensitive targets retain predictable non-breaking interactions and defer unverified break fallback. The schematic bridges report any overlapping subregions as a conflict, including overlaps whose target states may be identical. A change to schematic contents that leaves placement bounds unchanged may not invalidate a frozen target. Query and preview are bounded partial reads, not full-domain aggregate results. These gaps prevent claiming the complete 26.2/26.3 acceptance matrix.
